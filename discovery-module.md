# Discovery Module

## Objective

Define the search, then collect current candidates as structured records.

## Search brief

Write one line before searching. Example:

"Fully funded PhD programs and visiting fellowships in literary or cultural studies in the UK, Europe, and North America for an Indian humanities postgraduate, starting autumn."

## Opportunity types to search

- PhD studentships and doctoral programmes
- Visiting research fellowships
- Archive and library grants
- Language learning grants
- Summer schools and methods schools, including digital humanities
- Conference travel grants
- Journal and publishing fellowships
- Teaching assistantships
- Exchange programs, research internships, and jobs

## Where to look first

1. The programme's own pages
2. University department and graduate school pages
3. National funding bodies and government scholarship portals
4. Cultural institutes and area studies centres
5. Professional associations and research networks

Use aggregator pages only to find leads. Confirm everything on an official page.

## Search method

- Run separate searches per category and per region. Do not combine them into one query.
- Keep queries short and specific. Add the current year for deadlines.
- Search again with different terms if a query returns nothing useful.
- Record the date each page was checked.

## Output

A list of candidate records in the format defined in SKILL.md, section 7.

## Restrictions

Do not present a university programme and its separate scholarship as one record. Do not include a lead without a source URL.
