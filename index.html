About the project

Global Opportunity Navigator is a modular LLM skill that turns "I want to study or work abroad" into a short, verified, ranked list with deadlines and a dated plan.

What it does

  • Builds your profile: level, field, interests, citizenship, goal and window.
  • Searches current official pages for PhDs, scholarships, fellowships, archive grants, summer schools, exchanges, internships and jobs.
  • Removes what you are not eligible for and says why.
  • Ranks the rest and labels each one reach, target or safe.
  • Gives a timeline back from each deadline, a document checklist and your next three actions.

How it works

Eight modules work in sequence: Profile, Discovery, Filter, Ranking, Verification, Action Plan, Application Support and Output. Six switches shape each search: Fully funded only, Open deadlines only, Strict source verification, Include application fees, Include rolling deadlines and Balanced mix.

The skill never invents a programme, deadline, amount or link. If it cannot verify a detail, it marks it unverified and gives the official URL. It flags any scheme that asks for a fee to be selected.

Example queries

  • Find fully funded PhD programs in cultural studies or sociology abroad.
  • Which fellowships can an Indian humanities researcher apply for to visit an archive overseas?
  • Make a month by month plan for applying to PhD programs in Europe.
